# mortgage-dividend-calculator
# Mortgage + ETF Cash Flow Calculator

A mobile-friendly personal finance calculator for simulating:

- Mortgage repayments
- House affordability
- ETF / dividend income
- Monthly cash flow
- Editable household expenses

Built with:
- Pure HTML
- Vanilla JavaScript
- TailwindCSS CDN

No backend required.

---

# Features

## Main Calculator

Interactive sliders for:

- Total savings
- House price
- Initial deposit
- Annual salary
- Mortgage interest rate
- Mortgage term
- Dividend yield

Outputs:

- Mortgage amount
- Monthly mortgage repayment
- Remaining investment amount
- Monthly dividend income
- Estimated monthly take-home salary
- Net monthly cash flow

---

## Cash Flow Details Page

Editable monthly expenses:

- Council tax
- Utilities
- Insurance
- Maintenance
- Internet
- Food
- Transport
- Family / miscellaneous

Updates the final cash flow instantly.
