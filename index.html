UK Repayment Mortgage

M = P × [ r(1+r)^n ] / [ (1+r)^n − 1 ]
  • M = Monthly repayment
  • P = Principal (house price − deposit)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total months (years × 12)

UK Take-Home Estimate

Monthly take-home = Annual salary × 0.68 ÷ 12

Approximates income after 20% basic-rate Income Tax and ~12% National Insurance for typical earners. For higher earners, actual take-home will differ.

Dividend Income

Monthly dividend = (Savings − Deposit) × (Yield% ÷ 100) ÷ 12

Net Monthly Cash Flow

Net = Take-home + Dividends − Mortgage repayment
